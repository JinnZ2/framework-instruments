# Unfold Addendum — Pre-Adoption and Normalization Audit

CC0 1.0 Universal. Added 2026-09-06.

## Addition

Append the following probe after the frame verdict:

> **Step 10 — Pre-Adoption and Normalization Audit.** Identify the technologies, institutions, authorities, targets, harms, and legitimacy claims the question asks the reader to accept before deliberation. Separate textual presupposition from normalization effect and author intent. Compare relevant historical outcomes and excluded alternatives only with sourced, materially comparable evidence.

## Four Distinct Claims

| Claim | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|
| **Textual presupposition** | The words and grammar of the dilemma show that a condition is treated as given. |
| **Normalization effect** | A stated audience and mechanism explain how accepting the frame could make a practice appear ordinary, necessary, or legitimate. |
| **Historical recurrence** | Sourced cases are materially comparable in actors, technology, setting, decision conditions, and measured outcomes. |
| **Intentional propaganda** | External evidence establishes authorship, purpose, target audience, selection or distribution strategy, and persuasive intent. |

A text can have a normalization effect without being created as propaganda. Presupposition is not evidence of motive.

## Questions

- What must the reader accept before choosing either option?
- Which technology, authority, target designation, harm category, or institutional arrangement is already treated as legitimate or inevitable?
- Which prior question—whether adoption or authority is justified—has been moved outside the frame?
- Who is the audience, and through what mechanism could the framing alter acceptance?
- Is there evidence about who authored, selected, commissioned, or distributed the dilemma and for what purpose?
- Have materially comparable events occurred, and what were their denominators, outcomes, uncertainties, and sources?
- Does the frame omit observed failure rates, false positives, replacement effects, delayed harms, or alternatives documented in those cases?
- Is technological capability assumed in one part of the scenario while adjacent alternatives are frozen without evidence?
- What evidence would show that the dilemma is a neutral stress test rather than a persuasive framing device?

## Historical Evidence Contract

Historical claims identify sources, case-selection rules, relevant denominators, outcome definitions, uncertainty, and differences from the present scenario. A list of adverse anecdotes cannot establish typical effect, inevitability, or expected value. Classified, incomplete, or disputed records are marked accordingly.

The audit must not design, recommend, or operationalize harmful weapons, coercive systems, surveillance, or evasion of human control. Alternatives remain at the level needed to test whether the frame is exhaustive and lawful.

## Nulls

- If the text does not presuppose the disputed adoption or authority, no pre-adoption finding is present.
- If no audience or mechanism is specified, normalization effect remains `unknown`.
- If authorship, purpose, selection, and distribution evidence is absent, propaganda intent remains `unknown`.
- If historical cases are not materially comparable or lack usable denominators, recurrence and effectiveness claims are not evaluated.
- If the omitted adoption question was already settled through a valid process and remains reviewable, pre-adoption may be legitimate.
- If Step 10 makes every hypothetical involving a deployed technology into propaganda, the detector is measuring suspicion and is void.

## Scope

This step analyzes framing. It does not diagnose authors, infer hidden motives, establish the legality of a technology, decide target status, or replace historical, legal, and policy research.
